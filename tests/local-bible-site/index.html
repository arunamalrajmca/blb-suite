BLB Suite Local Bible Reference Smoke Test

Manual test page for Double-click, Alt+B, and Show on BLB. The page deliberately uses ordinary paragraphs and mixed reference forms so selection/gesture behavior can be tested against realistic DOM text.

Natural paragraphs

The Word became flesh and dwelt among us (John 1:14). For God so loved the world that he gave his only begotten Son (John 3:16), and Romans 5:8 also speaks of God's love.

The Bereans searched the Scriptures daily (Acts 17:11). Compare this with 2 Timothy 3:16-17, and remember that faith comes by hearing (Romans 10:17).

We walk by faith, not by sight (2 Corinthians 5:7), trusting the Lord with all our heart (Proverbs 3:5-6). Philippians 4:13 is another familiar reference to test in surrounding prose.

Manual smoke-test targets

  1. Double-click a reference token in each section.
  2. Select a complete reference and use Alt+B.
  3. Select a complete reference and use Show on BLB.
  4. Try references at the beginning, middle, and end of paragraphs.
  5. Try numbered books and aliases.
  6. Confirm ordinary numbered sentences do not become Bible references.
  7. Confirm the destination opened by the extension is BLB; this page is only the page under test.
