Blue Letter Bible Suite

Unified extension for BLB Auto Hyperlinker, BLB New Tab, KJV omnibox search, Bible-site redirects, and Webster's 1828 Bible-reference handling.

Omnibox shortcuts

  • Ruth: rut / rth
  • Song of Solomon: sol / sng
  • Jude: jud / jde
  • 1 John: 1jn / 1jo

Redirect sites

  • Bible.com
  • BibleGateway.com
  • BibleRef.com
  • BibleHub.com
  • KingJamesBibleOnline.org
  • KJBO.org
  • BiblePortal.com

John 3:16 is only an example. The redirector is designed for supported chapter/verse URL forms generally, not just that verse.

Sample URLs

  • https://www.bible.com/bible/1/JHN.3.16.KJV
  • https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+3%3A16&version=KJV
  • https://www.bibleref.com/John/3/John-3-16.html
  • https://biblehub.com/john/3-16.htm
  • https://www.kingjamesbibleonline.org/John-3-16/
  • https://kjbo.org/John-3-16/
  • https://www.bibleportal.com/verse-topic?v=john+3%3A16&version=KJV

Webster's 1828

Webster's 1828 remains a separate redirect behavior. Bible-reference links with class a.bible open the corresponding BLB KJV passage in a new tab.
