Blue Letter Bible Suite — Third-Party Notice for Case-Sensitive KJV Corpus

Source repository:
https://github.com/renniemaharaj/kjv-bible

The original-case KJV companion corpus in kjv-corpus-original-case.js is derived
from the repository above. The repository metadata identifies the repository as
MIT licensed. The KJV text is identified there as public domain in many
jurisdictions. The companion corpus preserves original capitalization and is
aligned to the Suite's existing 31,102-verse normalized corpus by
(book number, chapter, verse).

This notice does not alter the Suite's existing normalized KJV corpus or its
search/index architecture.
